Our story

Lucas Barra founded All In Coaching in 2016. Its home is on Prospect Street in the heart of La Jolla.

From the start, the firm has focused on real estate agents and brokers. That focus means coaching conversations stay on what moves a real estate business: pipeline, follow-up, time and leadership.
